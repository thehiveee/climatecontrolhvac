Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Climate Control HVAC ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through climatecontrolhvac.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you request an estimate, call us, or email us, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code or service address, the service you need, and any message you include.

Information collected automatically

With your consent, we use analytics cookies to collect information about how visitors use the Site, such as pages viewed, approximate location (city level), device and browser type, referring website, and clicks on our phone number or forms. We do not collect this until you accept analytics cookies.

Spam and security

To protect our forms from spam and abuse, we use a hidden spam-trap field, time checks and a bot-detection service, which may process your IP address and browser signals.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your request, schedule service and provide estimates.
  • To perform, invoice and follow up on work we carry out for you, including warranty service.
  • To improve the Site and understand which pages and services are most useful.
  • To prevent fraud, spam and misuse of the Site.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run our business and the Site — for example our website host, email delivery provider, analytics provider (Google Analytics) and bot-protection provider (Cloudflare Turnstile) — under terms that limit their use of it. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

4. Cookies and your choices

Essential cookies keep the Site working and remember your cookie choice. Analytics cookies are used only if you click "Accept analytics" in our cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

5. How long we keep it

We keep estimate requests and customer records for as long as needed to provide service, honor warranties and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations. Analytics data is retained according to our analytics settings and then deleted.

6. How we protect it

The Site uses HTTPS encryption. We apply reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information, consistent with New York's data-security requirements. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email info@climatecontrolny.com or call (212) 254-6283. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity.

8. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
