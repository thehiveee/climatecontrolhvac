Water heaters we install & repair

The right heater for your home — and your bill.

We'll match you to a properly sized unit that fits your household and your budget.

Standard tank

The most traditional and affordable option. Gas or electric, in a range of capacities.

Tankless (on-demand)

Heats water only when you need it — high efficiency, endless hot water and a fraction of the footprint.

Indirect tank

Works with your existing boiler to heat water, cutting the energy use and running cost of a separate burner.

Heat pump

Moves heat from the surrounding air instead of generating it — one of the most efficient electric options available.

Warning signs

Time to call if you see:

  • No hot waterOr it runs out much faster than it used to.
  • Water pooling at the baseA leaking tank can fail suddenly — call right away.
  • Rusty, muddy or smelly waterOften a sign of corrosion or sediment inside the tank.
  • Loud popping or rumblingSediment build-up is making the heater work harder.

Water heater FAQ

Common questions.

My water heater is leaking. What should I do?
If it's safe to reach, close the cold-water supply valve on top of the tank, then call us 24/7 at (212) 254-6283. Don't wait — leaks usually get worse.
Should I switch to tankless?
Tankless saves space and energy and never runs out, but the upfront cost is higher. We'll compare the options for your home in a free written quote.
Can you replace a water heater the same day?
We offer 24/7 emergency repair and replacement. Call and we'll tell you the earliest a technician can be there.
